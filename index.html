About

I work on Windows kernel-mode GPU graphics drivers at AMD in Markham. The heart of that work is crash and fault diagnostics and driver reliability: getting to the cause of a failure, and making the driver more stable. Alongside it, I architect multi-agent systems that use retrieval-augmented generation and tool-calling to automate root-cause analysis, generate high-fidelity debug reports, and offer real-time code-fix suggestions.

Kernel-mode drivers, crash diagnostics, and those agentic systems are where I go deep. I led a team to re-architect AMD crash dumps and drove key driver stability improvements for AMD Crash Defender. I spearheaded AI workflow adoption across engineering units, reducing mean time to resolution and generating $2M in annual savings, and collaborated with Microsoft on new developer tool features for Windows as part of GDC 2026.
